About / how I work

I would rather build the thing than describe it.

I am in the second year of a Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) in Mechanical Engineering at UNSW. The work I keep coming back to sits where mechanics, electronics and software have to agree with each other. A mount that has to hold an antenna steady while software drives it. An airframe that has to carry compute it was not originally sized for.

Seven years of competition robotics taught me three things that transferred: design against a fixed deadline, test without defending your own ideas, and keep a team moving after a bad qualifying round. That is the habit I bring to flight testing, space hardware and the software I put in front of real operators.
