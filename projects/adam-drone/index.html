The brief

Collect useful environmental data without walking the whole property.

Small scale farmers cannot instrument every corner of a paddock, and spot readings from one weather station do not describe a field. The goal is for ADA²M to follow a predefined coverage route and log environmental measurements along it. The engineering problem is packaging: an airframe, a flight controller, a long range link, onboard compute and a sensor payload all have to fit inside a mass and power budget that does not move.

Engineering work

Mechanical packaging and staged integration.

  1. Package the flight stack. Design the carbon fibre frame around the propulsion, control and compute hardware rather than fitting the hardware to a stock frame.
  2. Connect autonomy to flight. Integrate Jetson Nano inference with onboard decision making so the aircraft can act on what it sees.
  3. Design for range. Use a TBS Crossfire link for long-range control and telemetry. The operating envelope still requires flight validation.
  4. Test in steps. Move from short hops to stability tuning before expanding sensor and autonomy capability, so changes can be evaluated incrementally.
